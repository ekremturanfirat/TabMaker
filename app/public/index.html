Options

Good to know

  • Click any spot in the tab to jump the song there. The highlighted column follows the music.
  • The 3D hand view under the player shows the fretting hand the way you see it looking down at your guitar. The hand is see-through so you can see where every fingertip presses; the dashed outline is the thumb and palm behind the neck. 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky; a bright tip is the note you hear now, a dimmer one is a finger that stays down (in an arpeggio the whole chord shape stays pressed), an empty one hovers, ready; dashed rings = where the fingers go next. The line under it tells how to hold the hand for that passage. “hand”, under the drawing, switches to the flat fretboard or hides it.
  • The flat fretboard: the hand slides to its position and each finger presses its string (1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky; a long bar = barre); a ring at the nut = open string; dashed circles = the next notes.
  • Press Space to play / pause. Scroll yourself any time; “follow” waits a few seconds before it moves the page again.
  • Playing techniques: an arc joins a hammer-on or pull-off, a slanted line is a slide up / down, a curved arrow is a bend (½ = half step, full = whole step) and the arrow back down its release, a wavy line is vibrato. In the .txt they are written h, p, /, \, b, r and ~. They are guessed from the sound; set “Techniques” in Options to see more or none.
  • “Play the tab” plays back what the tab says, so you can compare it with the real song by ear. Tick “tab along” to hear the tab quietly on top of the song: wrong notes stand out.
  • “Compare with the song” plays the tab, listens to it the way it listened to the song, and matches the notes. Bar numbers turn green (sounds like the song), yellow or red (different); hover one to see which notes are missing or extra.
  • The AI hears notes, not hands: it picks the comfiest string/fret for each note, which may differ from the player’s. If the song is a video (a link, or an uploaded video file), the video shows up above the tab: pause where the neck is clearly visible, press Mark the neck and click the nut and the 12th fret wire (blue lines appear on the fret wires; click a third wire if they drift). Set the capo if there is one (orange line), then press Follow the hand: a hand model finds the fretting hand in every frame, and the tab keeps the hand where the player had it. Green dots = the fingertips it sees; the dashed orange line = where the tab puts the index finger. After a cut to another camera angle, mark the neck there too.
  • Full-band songs: vocals, bass and keys get picked up too. Use “Melody only” or “Bass only” and the sensitivity slider to clean it up.
Notes are detected with Spotify's Basic Pitch model (Apache 2.0).
