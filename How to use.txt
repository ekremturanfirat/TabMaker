TabMaker - turns songs into guitar tab
=====================================

FIRST TIME ON A COMPUTER (Windows)
  1. Put this TabMaker folder anywhere (for example in Documents).
  2. Double-click "Start TabMaker".
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway" (only once).
     The first time it may install Python and Deno. Click "Yes" if Windows asks.
     If the black window says "double-click Start TabMaker again", do that.
  3. TabMaker opens in your browser.

EVERY TIME AFTER THAT
  Double-click "Start TabMaker" in this folder.

MAKING A TAB
  Paste a song link and click "Make tab". Or upload a song file.
  Space = play / pause. Click anywhere in the tab to jump there.
  The fretboard under the player shows where your fingers go, live.

STOPPING
  Close the black "TabMaker" window.

GIVING IT TO A FRIEND
  Send them this link: https://github.com/ekremturanfirat/tabmaker
  (green "Code" button -> "Download ZIP"). Or double-click "Make a copy for a friend":
  it makes a clean TabMaker.zip on your Desktop (without your downloaded songs).

TabMaker - sarkilari gitar tab'ina cevirir
==========================================

BIR BILGISAYARDA ILK KEZ (Windows)
  1. TabMaker klasorunu istedigin yere koy (ornegin Belgeler).
  2. "Start TabMaker" dosyasina cift tikla.
     Windows "Windows bilgisayarinizi korudu" derse "Ek bilgi" -> "Yine de calistir"a tikla (bir kez).
     Ilk seferde Python ve Deno kurulabilir. Windows sorarsa "Evet"e tikla.
     Siyah pencere "double-click Start TabMaker again" derse, tekrar cift tikla.
  3. TabMaker tarayicida acilir.

SONRAKI HER SEFER
  Bu klasordeki "Start TabMaker"a cift tikla.

TAB YAPMAK
  Sarki linkini yapistir, "Make tab"e bas. Ya da sarki dosyasi yukle.
  Bosluk tusu = oynat / durdur. Tab'da bir yere tiklarsan sarki oraya gider.
  Oynaticinin altindaki klavye (fretboard) parmaklarin nereye gidecegini canli gosterir.

KAPATMAK
  Siyah "TabMaker" penceresini kapat.

ARKADASINA VERMEK
  Su linki gonder: https://github.com/ekremturanfirat/tabmaker
  (yesil "Code" butonu -> "Download ZIP"). Ya da "Make a copy for a friend"e cift tikla:
  Masaustune temiz bir TabMaker.zip yapar (indirdigin sarkilar olmadan).
